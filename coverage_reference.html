Dynamic Cohort Design Rationale

Key decisions from the October 28, 2026 call, with supporting evidence from the literature.

No minimum enrollment requirement

ASD adults cycle between Medicaid and Medicare as their waiver and disability status changes. A 12-month continuous enrollment filter would exclude an estimated 50 to 60 percent of the ASD cohort. Under the dynamic open-cohort design, person-time accrues only in enrolled months; enrollment gaps suspend but do not terminate follow-up.

Open cohort with dynamic entry

Beneficiaries enter the ASD cohort when their second qualifying F84.x claim appears (30+ days after the first), following the Burke 2014 and Vivanti 2025 ascertainment standard. Entry is permitted in any calendar year from 2008 onward, not only at a fixed baseline date, maximizing sample size across ICD-9 to ICD-10 transition years.

Dual enrollment and SES

Dual-enrolled ASD adults are not necessarily low-income. Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers carry higher or no income limits, so Medicaid eligibility via an HCBS developmental disability waiver does not imply poverty. The DUAL_ELGBL_MONS flag in MBSF identifies this population but does not bring Medicaid claims into the FFS environment.

Dementia ascertainment

Two validated claims-based approaches are available and closely related. Vivanti et al. 2025 used the Bynum algorithm (McCarthy / Grodstein 2022): ICD-10 codes F01.xx, F02.xx, F03.xx, G30.xx in Part A/B claims plus Part D dementia medications. Tobi's DREAM study (Desai et al. 2025) uses diagnosis codes only — 1 inpatient or 2 outpatient claims — with no Part D component. Both require a clean washout at cohort entry. The team protocol will specify which standard to apply; the choice should be aligned across the group, particularly with Tobi.

Key References

Citations backing the analytical choices in this document, organized by topic. All PMIDs and DOIs can be verified in PubMed.

ASD Ascertainment
Burke JP, Jain A, Yang W, Kelly JP, Kaiser M, Becker L, Lawer L, Newschaffer CJ. Does a claims diagnosis of autism mean a true case? Autism. 2014;18(3):321–330. doi:10.1177/1362361312467709
Establishes the PPV standard for 2+ claims with ASD diagnosis codes at least 30 days apart. Foundational criterion for the study cohort entry definition.
Vivanti G, Lee W, Ventimiglia J, Tao S, Lyall K, Shea LL. Prevalence of Dementia Among US Adults With Autism Spectrum Disorder. JAMA Netw Open. 2025;8(1):e2453691. PMID: 39745705. doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.53691
Applied the 2-claim F84.x ascertainment standard to 114,582 dual-enrolled Medicare-Medicaid beneficiaries (2014–2016). Reported dementia prevalence 2.56× higher in ASD adults vs. matched controls; used Bynum dementia algorithm.
Dementia Ascertainment
Grodstein F, Chang C-H, Capuano AW, Power MC, Marquez DX, Barnes LL, Bennett DA, James BD, Bynum JPW. Identification of Dementia in Recent Medicare Claims Data, Compared With Rigorous Clinical Assessments. J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med Sci. 2022;77(6):1272–1278. PMID: 34919685. PMCID: PMC9159666. doi:10.1093/gerona/glab377
Validates ICD-10 algorithm (F01.xx, F02.xx, F03.xx, G30.xx in Part A/B claims, plus Part D dementia medications) against rigorous clinical assessments. With McCarthy et al. 2022, constitutes the "Bynum algorithm" — the standard used by Vivanti et al. 2025.
McCarthy EP, Chang CH, Tilton N, Kabeto MU, Langa KM, Bynum JPW. Validation of Claims Algorithms to Identify Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias. J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med Sci. 2022;77(6):1261–1271. PMID: 34919686. PMCID: PMC9159657. doi:10.1093/gerona/glab373
Companion validation paper; together with Grodstein et al. 2022 above, defines the claims algorithm applied by Vivanti et al. 2025 and proposed for this study.
Desai RJ, Varma VR, Mahesri M, Lee SB, Freedman A, Gerhard T, Segal J, Vine S, Ritchey MBE, Horton DB, Thambisetty M. Population-Based Validation Results From the Drug Repurposing for Effective Alzheimer's Medicines (DREAM) Study. Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics. 2025. (epub ahead of print) doi:10.1002/cpt.3583
Tobi Gerhard's DREAM study — a related dementia ascertainment approach: ICD-10 diagnosis codes in 1 inpatient or 2 outpatient claims, washout = full baseline period, no Part D drug component. Used in Medicare + CPRD. The team should confirm whether the DREAM or Bynum standard will be applied.
ASD–Dementia Epidemiology
Vivanti G, Tao S, Lyall K, Robins DL, Shea LL. The prevalence and incidence of early-onset dementia among adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Autism Research. 2021;14(10):2189–2199. doi:10.1002/aur.2590
Medicaid Analytic eXtract 2008–2012 (n = 12,648 ASD-only adults). ASD-only HR 1.96 (95% CI 1.69–2.28) for dementia vs. general Medicaid population; mean age at dementia diagnosis ~49 years. Primary precedent for the autism and dementia risk hypothesis.
[Karolinska Institutet authors]. Association between autism and dementia across generations: evidence from a family study of the Swedish population. Molecular Psychiatry. 2025. PMC12436158. doi:10.1038/s41380-025-03045-6
Swedish national registry data (Sven's arm). Population-based family study documenting the autism–dementia association across generations. Provides international validity context for the US Medicare findings.
US Medicare Enrollment
Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). Medicare Beneficiaries at a Glance, Data Year 2021. CMS; 2021.
63.9M total Medicare beneficiaries; ~57% in FFS (~36.4M); ~13% under age 65 (~8.3M disability-enrolled). Basis for comparison group pool size estimates used in this document.
